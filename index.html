Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions before you get started — answered honestly.

Will the replies sound robotic?

No. Biashara-Assistant is trained on your business — your menu, services, prices, and tone. Customers get natural, helpful replies that sound like your team, not a generic chatbot.

What if a customer needs to speak to a real person?

The assistant handles routine questions and bookings, then hands off to you or your team when needed. You stay in control — nothing goes out without your approval during setup.

Do I need technical skills to set this up?

Not at all. Fill out a short form and we'll walk you through connecting your channels — WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, and more. Most businesses are live within a day.

Are my accounts and data safe?

Yes. You keep full control of your existing accounts and numbers. We never share your data with third parties, and you can pause or stop the service at any time.

How fast can I go live?

Most setups take less than 24 hours after you submit your details. We'll contact you on WhatsApp to confirm everything before going live.

How do I get started?

Fill out the form below or message us on WhatsApp. There's no payment required upfront — we'll set up your assistant and show you how it works before you commit.
