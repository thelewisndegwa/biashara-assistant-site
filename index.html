Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions before you get started — answered honestly.

Will the replies sound robotic?

No. Biashara-Assistant is trained on your business — your menu, services, prices, and tone. Customers get natural, helpful replies that sound like your team, not a generic chatbot.

What if a customer needs to speak to a real person?

The assistant handles routine questions and bookings, then hands off to you or your team when needed. You stay in control — nothing goes out without your approval during setup.

Do I need technical skills to set this up?

Not at all. Fill out a short form and we'll walk you through connecting your channels — WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, and more — when you're ready to go live.

Are my accounts and data safe?

Yes. You keep full control of your existing accounts and numbers. We never share your data with third parties, and you can pause or stop the service at any time.

How fast can I go live?

It depends on your channels and how your business is set up. After you submit the form, we'll contact you on WhatsApp, walk through what's involved, and agree on a timeline together.

What is Business Biashara CRM?

Business Biashara CRM is the customer management plugin built into Biashara-Assistant. It automatically saves every inquiry as a contact, stores full conversation history across all channels, and lets you track leads through your sales pipeline — so nothing gets lost in chat.

Fill out the form below or message us on WhatsApp. We'll follow up to learn about your business, explain how setup works, and answer your questions before you commit.
